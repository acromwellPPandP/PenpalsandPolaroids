# Penpals & Polaroids - Project Rules

## Mission

Penpals & Polaroids exists to bring people from around the world together through thoughtful letters and meaningful photographs.

The platform encourages genuine international friendships, cultural exchange and curiosity about everyday life across different countries and cultures.

Every design and development decision should support this mission.

---

## Core Values

- Slow communication over instant messaging.
- Authentic stories over curated social media.
- Meaningful friendships over popularity.
- Cultural discovery over endless scrolling.
- Thoughtful conversations over short messages.
- Quality over quantity.

---

## Product Principles

Every feature should answer one question:

"Does this help people build genuine international friendships?"

If the answer is no, reconsider the feature.

The experience should always feel calm, welcoming, personal and intentional.

---

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Every letter must include exactly one photograph.
- A letter cannot be sent without a photograph.
- Profiles are always called "Passports".
- Conversations are always called "Scrapbooks".
- Every friendship should feel like a shared journey.
- The platform should never encourage popularity contests or addictive engagement.

Never include:

- Likes
- Followers
- Public popularity metrics
- Infinite scrolling
- Trending pages
- Viral content mechanics

---

## Design Language

The interface should feel like opening a travel journal.

Visual inspiration:

- Vintage passports
- Polaroid photographs
- Handwritten letters
- Airmail envelopes
- Wooden desks
- Travel journals
- Soft paper textures
- Passport stamps
- Warm neutral colours
- Clean Scandinavian-inspired layouts

The feeling should be:

Warm
Nostalgic
Friendly
Relaxing
Elegant
Timeless

Avoid:

- Neon colours
- Heavy gradients
- Dark cyber aesthetics
- Gaming UI
- Corporate dashboards
- Overly cluttered layouts

## Passport

Every Passport should include:

- Profile photo
- Name
- Country
- Languages
- Interests
- Short biography
- Member since
- Letters sent
- Polaroids shared
- Countries connected

The Passport should feel like a real travel document rather than a social media profile.

---

## Scrapbooks

Each friendship automatically creates a shared scrapbook.

Every exchanged letter becomes a scrapbook page containing:

- The letter
- The accompanying Polaroid
- Date sent
- Country of origin

Users should feel like they are collecting memories rather than messages.

---

## Community

Penpals & Polaroids is a community built around kindness, curiosity and respect.

Members are encouraged to:

- Learn about different cultures
- Share everyday life
- Build lasting friendships
- Exchange thoughtful letters
- Celebrate differences

The platform should promote respectful interactions and provide clear tools for reporting, blocking and moderation to help maintain a safe environment.

---

## Future Features

Potential future additions:

- Passport stamps for first letters exchanged with new countries
- Interactive world map showing countries connected
- Friendship anniversaries
- Cultural conversation prompts
- Seasonal stationery themes
- Time capsule letters
- Optional printed postcard service
- Language learning support
- Translation assistance

Future features should enhance thoughtful communication rather than increase time spent in the app.

---

## Development Guidelines

Build features incrementally.

Keep components reusable.

Prioritise accessibility.

Maintain responsive layouts.

Preserve Supabase authentication and database structure unless changes are required.

Explain significant code changes after each completed task.

When implementing any new feature, consult this document first and ensure every decision supports the mission, design language and values of Penpals & Polaroids.
